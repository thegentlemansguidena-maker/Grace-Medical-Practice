## The eight topics (authored)

1. **Vibe:** soft, unhurried, warm, quiet authority. References: early winter
   light on the Auas hills; a clean linen sheet folded on an examination couch;
   the hush of a waiting room before the first patient.
2. **Journey:** arrive at the door, meet the doctor, take a breath, see what the
   practice helps with, find the building, take the details home.
3. **Energy:** calm the whole way, with one held, slower moment in the middle.
   Never loud.
4. **Feeling, and the one moment:** see the curve below. The moment is the breath.
5. **What no other site does:** it asks you to take a slow breath, the way a
   doctor does with a stethoscope on your back, and the page breathes with you
   as you scroll.
6. **Range:** premium-minimal, softened. Editorial warmth, not clinical white.
7. **World:** distinct scenes, not one continuous flight.
8. **Assets:** none supplied. Budget is 52 kie.ai credits, so at most two
   generated stills, no video. Depth beyond that is built in CSS.

## Feeling curve

1. Arrival: **ease**. A soft dawn over the hills, the name sitting behind an
   aloe, morning veil drifting.
2. Welcome: **trust**. Plain sentences, a named doctor, nothing sold.
3. Breath: **stillness** (the peak). A pinned ring swells as you scroll. "Breathe
   in." "Hold." "And out." The rest of the page falls away.
4. Care: **reassurance**. A light card rail of everyday reasons to visit.
5. Find us: **orientation**. The address set large, an unhurried wipe.
6. Take home: **readiness**. A card you can save to your phone.

## The peak

"The website literally made me take a breath before I booked." Lives in act 3,
the largest span on the page, with a quieter flow act in front of it.

**Tell-someone sentence:** It's the site where the doctor asks you to breathe,
before you even walk in.

## Authored silence

The breath act's "Hold" window is intentional stillness (the ring stays full,
no copy change). It is not dead scroll.
